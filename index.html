About the Studio

Spaces shaped by light, texture and quiet detail.

Nour Zaatari Studio is an interior design practice devoted to creating homes and spaces that feel effortless to live in. Every project begins with listening — to the people who will inhabit it, to the architecture, and to the light that moves through it.

Our work balances warmth and restraint: a considered palette of natural materials, tailored furniture and pieces that carry meaning, layered with care until a space feels entirely your own.
